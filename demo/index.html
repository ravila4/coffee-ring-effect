coffee rings — evaporation-driven particle deposition

Each stain is a Monte-Carlo run of Deegan's coffee-ring physics: solute particles advected by the outward capillary flow of a drying droplet whose contact line is pinned, jamming where they reach it. Stick-slip recession of the contact line leaves secondary inner rings; whatever is still suspended at dry-out settles as the mottled interior. Click a stain to re-brew it.
