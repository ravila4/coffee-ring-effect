Coffee stains as a texture

One mug ring and four droplets, generated once and painted onto a transparent canvas laid over the page. Nothing was composited into the artwork ahead of time: the ruled paper and the text below are ordinary page content, and the stains multiply over them.

Field notes, Tuesday

The edge of a puddle sticks to the surface under it rather than shrinking inward as it dries. Water leaves fastest right at that edge, so liquid flows outward from the middle to replace what evaporates, carrying the suspended particles with it. They jam against the edge and stay there.

That is the whole of the coffee-ring effect, and it is why the mark a mug leaves is a ring and not a disk. Set the cup down twice and you get two rings, crossing where the second landed off the first.
